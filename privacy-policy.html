1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use the Smart Riddles mobile application on Android devices via the Google Play Store.

2. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information (None)

  • Smart Riddles does not require account creation or registration.
  • We do not collect, store, or sell personal identifiers such as your real name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

B. Local Gameplay Data

  • Game progress, coin balance, solved level indices, hint unlocks, and daily skip timestamps are stored exclusively on your device via Android SharedPreferences.
  • This data is never transmitted to or stored on external servers by Tedrasoft.

C. Technical & Diagnostic Information (Third-Party SDKs)

Third-party services (such as Google Mobile Ads / AdMob and Google Play Services) may automatically collect diagnostic and device telemetry:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version.
  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and performance metrics.
  • IP address (used to infer coarse location at country or city level).

3. Third-Party Services & Monetization

Smart Riddles integrates standard Google services subject to their respective privacy terms:

  1. Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases):
    • When you purchase coin bundles, the financial transaction is handled directly and securely by Google Play.
    • Tedrasoft does not receive, view, or retain payment card information or banking details.
    • Google Privacy Policy
  2. Google Mobile Ads / AdMob (Rewarded Video Ads):
    • Optional rewarded video ads grant in-game coins (+20 coins) upon completion.
    • AdMob may collect and process device identifiers in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.
    • Google AdMob Privacy Information

4. Permissions Requested

  • Permissions related to internet access: Required to communicate with Google Play Services and load rewarded video advertisements.
  • Permissions related to advertising and advertising ids: Required to show ads.
  • Permissions related to phone vibrating: Used exclusively to provide tactile haptic feedback during letter button interactions.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Smart Riddles is designed for all audiences. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies:

  • We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion

Because all game records are saved locally on your device, you maintain full control over your data:

  • To delete all game history and coin data, navigate to: Settings > Apps > Smart Riddles > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Alternatively, uninstalling the application completely erases all locally saved game files.
