Privacy Policy for TempoBite

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

TempoBite ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application (TempoBite) handles your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect and Process

A. Camera & Visual Data (Facial Landmarks)

  • Purpose: TempoBite uses your device's front-facing camera in real-time to analyze facial landmarks (such as jaw movement and lip shape) using on-device machine learning (MediaPipe) to track chewing cadence, calculate chew rates, and estimate bite ingestion.
  • On-Device Processing: All video and image processing occurs 100% locally on your device. No video frames, photographs, facial geometry, or biometric templates are ever recorded, saved, transmitted, or uploaded to any external or cloud servers.

B. Local Meal Statistics & Preferences

  • Purpose: To display your meal history, chewing speed (CPM), meal duration, and bite count estimates, and to remember your timer/validator preferences.
  • Storage: This data is stored exclusively on your device in local storage (SharedPreferences). We do not have access to your meal history or personal settings.

2. How We Use Information

Since all data processing and storage happen entirely on your device:

  • We do not collect, store, or process personally identifiable information (PII).
  • We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or user profiling.

3. Third-Party Services and SDKs

TempoBite operates completely offline with respect to data collection. We do not integrate third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or cloud databases that collect user data.

4. Permissions Required

TempoBite requests the following permission solely for core functionality:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Required exclusively for real-time on-device face mesh tracking during active meal tracking sessions. You can disable camera access at any time through your device settings (note that AI Vision Tracking requires camera access to function).

5. Children's Privacy

TempoBite does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected or transmitted from the app, it is safe for users of all ages practicing mindful eating.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this document with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
