Privacy Policy for Mismatched (iOS)

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Developer: Dragos Cosmineanu / Tedrasoft

App Name: Mismatched (Mismatched: Picture Puzzle)

Apple ID / App Store ID: 972429993

Bundle Identifier: com.tedrasoft.mismatched

Mismatched is a visual category puzzle game offering optional in-app purchases and advertisements. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled, collected, and protected when you use our iOS application, in full compliance with Apple's App Store Review Guidelines, App Tracking Transparency (ATT), and global privacy regulations (GDPR and CCPA/CPRA).

1. Information Collection and Processing

We respect your privacy and adhere to the principle of data minimization. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

A. Advertisements (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob & Google UMP)

The app displays banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads via Google Mobile Ads. Depending on your consent choices and iOS privacy permissions:

  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS 14.5 and later, we request permission via the system ATT prompt before accessing the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). If you choose "Ask App not to Track", non-personalized ads are served, and no cross-app tracking occurs.
  • Consent Management (Google UMP): Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland are presented with a certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) dialog compliant with the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF). You can update your choices at any time by tapping the Settings (gear icon) on the main menu.
  • Ad Data Collected by Google: When permitted, Google AdMob may process device identifiers, coarse IP address (to infer approximate country/city), diagnostic performance metrics, and ad interaction metrics to deliver and measure ad performance, prevent click fraud, and detect invalid traffic.
  • For more information on how Google handles advertising data, consult Google's Privacy & Terms.

B. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit)

The app provides optional non-consumable and consumable in-app purchases (such as Hint packs, Winter Pack, and No Ads).

  • Payment Processing: All financial transactions, billing details, and payment card information are processed directly and securely by Apple via StoreKit 2. The developer never receives, handles, or stores any payment information or credit card numbers.
  • Entitlement Verification: The app accesses local StoreKit transaction receipts on your device solely to unlock features you purchased and restore them across your Apple devices linked to your Apple ID.

C. Local Game Storage (UserDefaults and Documents)

Your gameplay progress (unlocked levels), remaining hint balance, music preferences, and purchase entitlements are saved locally on your device (in NSUserDefaults and standard local application documents). This data is stored entirely on your hardware and is never transmitted to any external private database or third-party server.

2. Third-Party Services & Privacy Links

The app integrates the following third-party developer frameworks:

  • Apple StoreKit & App Store Services: Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) & User Messaging Platform (UMP): Google Privacy Policy

3. Apple Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy)

In accordance with Apple requirements for iOS apps:

  • No User Tracking Without Consent: Tracking is disabled by default unless explicitly granted through ATT.
  • API Usage Declarations: NSUserDefaults is declared under approved reason CA92.1 strictly for local app settings and player preferences.
  • No Fingerprinting: We do not use device signals to create persistent fingerprints.

4. Children's Privacy

Mismatched is intended for general audiences and puzzle enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal data from a child has inadvertently been processed, please contact us immediately to remove it.

5. Your Rights and Data Choices

  • Advertising Choices: You can adjust ad tracking permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Mismatched. You can also re-open the Consent Management settings directly in the app using the gear button on the home screen.
  • Data Deletion: Because game progress is stored entirely locally on your device, uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or platform features. The revised policy will always be posted with an updated effective date.
