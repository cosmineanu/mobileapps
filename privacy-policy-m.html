Privacy Policy for Mismatched

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Developer: Dragos Cosmineanu

App Name: Mismatched (Mismatched Images)

Package Name: com.tedrasoft.mismatched

Mismatched app was built as a free-to-play puzzle game with optional in-app purchases and advertisements. This file outlines how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

We collect minimal information to provide, support, and improve your gameplay experience. The types of data collected depend on your interactions with the app:

A. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads). AdMob may collect and use certain data to serve ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • IP address and device identifiers
  • Approximate location data (derived from IP)
  • Ad interaction data (ad views, clicks)

Google AdMob uses this information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. In compliance with Google Play requirements, users in applicable regions are presented with a consent management prompt (via Google User Messaging Platform / UMP) to choose between personalized and non-personalized advertising.

B. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The app offers digital goods and upgrades for purchase, such as puzzle hints, the Winter Pack level expansion, and No Ads removal, processed securely via Google Play Billing.

  • Transaction Data: Google Play handles all payment card details and financial transactions. Developer does not collect, process, or store your credit card or financial account numbers.
  • Entitlement Verification: We verify purchase tokens locally and via Google Play to ensure proper delivery of purchased items and ad-free status.

C. Local Game Progress & Preferences

Your game progress (unlocked levels), hint balance, ratings status, and purchase entitlements are stored securely in local device storage (SharedPreferences) to maintain your gameplay state across sessions.

2. Third-Party Services

We integrate trusted third-party SDKs that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob & UMP SDK

3. Children's Privacy

Mismatched is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

4. Data Security

We value your trust in providing your information. While we use commercially acceptable means of protecting data, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
